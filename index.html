1. Traditional SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Simple Explanation:

"Like making sure your business is listed on page 1 of the directory when someone types exact keywords on Google."

Real-World Example:

When someone searches "best salon in Pune", your website shows up among the blue links on Google Search.

2. AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Simple Explanation:

"Structuring your business data so voice assistants (Siri/Alexa) and Google Snippets directly read out YOUR business details as the single right answer."

Real-World Example:

When someone asks Siri "What time does Jawed Habib Keshav Nagar open today?", Siri reads your exact opening hours directly without making them click a site.

3. GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

Simple Explanation:

"Teaching Large Language Models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) who you are, what you offer, and why they should recommend you over competitors."

Real-World Example:

When a user prompts ChatGPT "Recommend a reliable dental clinic near Viman Nagar", ChatGPT writes a paragraph explicitly citing your clinic and linking your booking page.

4. Local Media & Automation

Micro-Websites & WhatsApp Automation

Simple Explanation:

"Connecting single-page lightning-fast micro-websites with automated 24/7 WhatsApp customer chat & Google Map review collection."

Real-World Example:

A customer taps your WhatsApp link on Google Maps, receives an instant automated booking reply, and books a salon or clinic appointment in seconds.
